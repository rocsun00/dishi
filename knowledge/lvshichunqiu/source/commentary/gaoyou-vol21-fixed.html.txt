</p><p>吕氏春秋第二十一卷　　　高氏訓解
</p><p>　雲間　宋<span class="skin-invert" typeof="mw:File/Frameless"><span><img alt="邦" src="//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/SKQS_3771.svg/20px-SKQS_3771.svg.png?utm_source=zh.wikisource.org&amp;utm_campaign=parser&amp;utm_content=thumbnail" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/SKQS_3771.svg/40px-SKQS_3771.svg.png?utm_source=zh.wikisource.org&amp;utm_campaign=parser&amp;utm_content=thumbnail 2x" data-file-width="365" data-file-height="365" /></span></span>㐅　范廷啓　徐益孫　何玉畏校
</p><p>　開春論第一
</p><p>　　開春
</p><p>一曰開春始雷則蟄蟲動矣<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r2531726">.mw-parser-output small.sfrac.nowrap{text-indent:initial}.mw-parser-output small.sfrac.nowrap>span{display:inline-block;vertical-align:middle;color:var(--color-destructive--visited,#996666)}.mw-parser-output small.sfrac.nowrap>span>span{display:block}.mw-parser-output .wst-dl-bracket{color:transparent;font-size:0px}</style><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>動蘇</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>時雨降則草木育
</p><p>矣<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>育長</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>飲食居處適則九竅百節千脉皆通利矣<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>通</span><span>利</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>不壅閉無</span><span>疾病矣</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>王者厚其徳積衆善而鳳皇聖人皆來至
</p><p>矣<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>雄曰鳳雌曰皇三代來至門庭周室至於山澤詩</span><span>云鳳皇鳴矣于彼髙岡此之謂也聖人皆來至謂</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>堯得夔龍稷契舜得益湯得</span><span>伊尹武丁得傅説之屬是也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>共伯和修其行好賢仁
</p><p>而海内皆以來為稽矣<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>共國伯爵夏時諸侯也以好</span><span>賢仁而人歸之皆以來附為</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span><span><span class="pagenum ws-pagenum" id="3" data-page-number="3" data-page-name="Page:Sibu Congkan0424-高誘-呂氏春秋-5-5.djvu/3" data-page-index="3" data-page-quality="1" title="Page:Sibu&#95;Congkan0424-%E9%AB%98%E8%AA%98-%E5%91%82%E6%B0%8F%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B-5-5.djvu/3"><span id="pageindex&#95;3" class="pagenum-inner ws-noexport">&#8203;</span></span></span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>稽遲</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>周厲之難天子曠絶<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>難厲王流于彘也周無天</span><span>子十一年故曰曠絶也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p>而天下皆來謂矣<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>謂天</span><span>子也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>以此言物之相應也故曰行
</p><p>也成也善説者亦然言盡理而得失利害定矣豈爲
</p><p>一人言哉<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>善説者大言天下之事得其分理愛之</span><span>不助憎之不枉故曰豈爲一人言哉</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>魏
</p><p>惠王死葬有日矣<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>孟子所見梁惠王也秦伐魏魏徙</span><span>都大梁梁在陳<span class="skin-invert" typeof="mw:File/Frameless"><span><img alt="留" src="//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/SKQS_3361.svg/20px-SKQS_3361.svg.png?utm_source=zh.wikisource.org&amp;utm_campaign=parser&amp;utm_content=thumbnail" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/SKQS_3361.svg/40px-SKQS_3361.svg.png?utm_source=zh.wikisource.org&amp;utm_campaign=parser&amp;utm_content=thumbnail 2x" data-file-width="280" data-file-height="280" /></span></span>浚儀西大梁城</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>是</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>天大雨雪至於牛目羣臣多諫於太子者曰雪甚
</p><p>如此而行葬民必甚疾之官費又恐不給<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>給足</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>請弛
</p><p>期更日<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>更改</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>太子曰爲人子者以民勞與官費用之
</p><p>故而不行先王之葬不義也子勿復言羣臣皆莫敢
</p><p>諫而以告犀首<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>犀首魏人公孫衍也佩五國</span><span>相印能合從連横號爲犀首</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>犀首曰<span><span class="pagenum ws-pagenum" id="4" data-page-number="4" data-page-name="Page:Sibu Congkan0424-高誘-呂氏春秋-5-5.djvu/4" data-page-index="4" data-page-quality="1" title="Page:Sibu&#95;Congkan0424-%E9%AB%98%E8%AA%98-%E5%91%82%E6%B0%8F%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B-5-5.djvu/4"><span id="pageindex&#95;4" class="pagenum-inner ws-noexport">&#8203;</span></span></span>
</p><p>吾未有以言之<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>未猶</span><span>無也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>是其唯惠公乎請告惠公<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>言唯</span><span>惠公</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>能諫之也惠公</span><span>惠王相惠施也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>惠公曰諾駕而見太子曰葬有日矣
</p><p>太子曰然惠公曰昔王季歷葬於渦山之尾欒水齧
</p><p>其墓見棺之前和<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>棺頭</span><span>曰和</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>文王曰譆先君必欲一見羣
</p><p>臣百姓也天故使欒水見之<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>見猶</span><span>出也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>於是出而為之張
</p><p>朝百姓皆見之三日而後更葬此文王之義也今葬
</p><p>有日矣而雪甚及牛目難以行太子為及日之故得
</p><p>無嫌於欲亟葬乎願太子易日先王必欲少<span class="skin-invert" typeof="mw:File/Frameless"><span><img alt="留" src="//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/SKQS_3361.svg/20px-SKQS_3361.svg.png?utm_source=zh.wikisource.org&amp;utm_campaign=parser&amp;utm_content=thumbnail" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/SKQS_3361.svg/40px-SKQS_3361.svg.png?utm_source=zh.wikisource.org&amp;utm_campaign=parser&amp;utm_content=thumbnail 2x" data-file-width="280" data-file-height="280" /></span></span>而撫
</p><p>社稷安黔首也故使雨雪甚因弛期而更為日此文
</p><p>王之義也若此而不為意者羞法文王也太子曰甚<span><span class="pagenum ws-pagenum" id="5" data-page-number="5" data-page-name="Page:Sibu Congkan0424-高誘-呂氏春秋-5-5.djvu/5" data-page-index="5" data-page-quality="1" title="Page:Sibu&#95;Congkan0424-%E9%AB%98%E8%AA%98-%E5%91%82%E6%B0%8F%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B-5-5.djvu/5"><span id="pageindex&#95;5" class="pagenum-inner ws-noexport">&#8203;</span></span></span>
</p><p>善敬弛期更擇葬日惠子不徒行説也又令魏太子
</p><p>未葬其先君而因有説文王之義説文王之義以示
</p><p>天下豈小功也哉韓氏城新城期十五日而成<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>韓氏</span><span>本都</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>𢎞農宜陽其後都頴川陽翟新城今</span><span>河南新城是也故戎蠻子之國也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>叚喬為司空有
</p><p>一縣後二日段喬執其吏而囚之囚者之子走告封
</p><p>人子髙曰唯先生能活臣父之死<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>子髙賢者也封人</span><span>田大夫職在封疆</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>故謂之封人周禮亦有封人之</span><span>官傳曰頴考叔為頴谷封人也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>願委之先生封人子
</p><p>髙曰諾乃見叚喬自扶而上城封人子髙左右望曰
</p><p>美哉城乎一大功矣子必有厚賞矣自古及今功若
</p><p>此其大也而能無有罪戮者未嘗有也封人子髙出<span><span class="pagenum ws-pagenum" id="6" data-page-number="6" data-page-name="Page:Sibu Congkan0424-高誘-呂氏春秋-5-5.djvu/6" data-page-index="6" data-page-quality="1" title="Page:Sibu&#95;Congkan0424-%E9%AB%98%E8%AA%98-%E5%91%82%E6%B0%8F%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B-5-5.djvu/6"><span id="pageindex&#95;6" class="pagenum-inner ws-noexport">&#8203;</span></span></span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>出去</span><span>也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>叚喬使人夜解其吏之束縛也而出之故曰封
</p><p>人子髙為之言也而匿已之為而為也叚喬聽而行
</p><p>之也匿已之行而行也説之行若此其精也封人子
</p><p>髙可謂善説矣叔嚮之弟羊舌虎善欒盈<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>欒盈晉大</span><span>夫欒書之</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>孫欒黶之</span><span>子懐子也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>欒盈有罪於晉晉誅羊舌虎叔嚮為之奴
</p><p>而朡<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>奴戮也律坐父兄没入為奴周禮曰其</span><span>奴男子入于罪𨽻此之謂也朡繫也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>祈奚曰
</p><p>吾聞小人得位不爭不祥<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>當諫君退之故</span><span>不爭不祥也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>君子在憂
</p><p>不救不祥<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>憂陒也當諌君免</span><span>之故不救不祥也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>乃往見范宣子而説也
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>祈奚髙梁伯之子祈黄羊也為范宣子</span><span>説叔嚮也范宣子范文子之子丐也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>曰聞善為國
</p><p>者賞不過而刑不慢賞過則懼及淫人刑慢則懼及<span><span class="pagenum ws-pagenum" id="7" data-page-number="7" data-page-name="Page:Sibu Congkan0424-高誘-呂氏春秋-5-5.djvu/7" data-page-index="7" data-page-quality="1" title="Page:Sibu&#95;Congkan0424-%E9%AB%98%E8%AA%98-%E5%91%82%E6%B0%8F%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B-5-5.djvu/7"><span id="pageindex&#95;7" class="pagenum-inner ws-noexport">&#8203;</span></span></span>
</p><p>君子與其不幸而過寧過而賞淫人毋過而刑君子
</p><p>故堯之刑也殛鮌於虞而用禹<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>殛誅也於舜用</span><span>禹禹鮌之子也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>周之
</p><p>刑也戮管蔡而相周公<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>管叔周公弟蔡叔其兄也二</span><span>人流言欲亂周室而戮之周</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>
</p><p><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>公相成王而</span><span>尹天下也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>不慢刑也宣子乃命吏出叔嚮救人之
</p><p>患者行危苦不避煩辱猶不能免今祈奚論先王之
</p><p>徳而叔向得免焉學豈可以已哉類多若此
</p><p>　　察賢
</p><p>二曰今有良醫於此治十人而起九人所以求之萬
</p><p>也<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r2531726" /><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〈</span><small class="sfrac nowrap"><span><span>以術之良故</span><span>人多求之也</span></span></small><span class="wst-dl-bracket">〉</span>故賢者之致功名也必乎良醫而君
